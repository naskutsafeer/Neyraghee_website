How It's Made

The Bilona Process

🥛
Step 01
Fresh Milk

Collected fresh from naturally raised cows every morning.

🫙
Step 02
Curd Setting

Milk is curdled naturally overnight using traditional starters.

🏺
Step 03
Bilona Churning

Curd is hand-churned with a wooden churner to extract butter.

✨
Step 04
Pure Ghee

Butter is slow-cooked on low flame until golden ghee is ready.
